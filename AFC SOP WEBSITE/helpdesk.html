BEFORE CONTACTING SUPPORT

Information to provide

  • Your name, branch and contact details.
  • The affected device or business service.
  • When the problem started and whether it is recurring.
  • The exact error message, where available.
  • The number of employees affected.
  • The business activities interrupted by the problem.
  • A relevant screenshot where useful, with sensitive information removed.
Keep your password private.

Never include passwords or authentication codes in a support request.

FROM REPORTING TO RESOLUTION

Help desk procedures

  • Report the issue: Employees must contact the help desk through the approved support email, telephone number or ticket system. Urgent incidents must be reported by telephone.
  • Provide information: The employee must provide their name, branch, contact details, affected device or service, error message and description of the business impact. Passwords must never be included.
  • Create a ticket: The Help Desk Technician must record the request, assign a unique ticket number and note the reporting time.
  • Acknowledge receipt: The help desk must give the employee the ticket number and explain the next steps.
  • Classify the request: The technician must identify whether the ticket concerns a service interruption, security incident or routine service request.
  • Set priority: Priority must reflect urgency and business impact. An outage affecting all branches or a suspected security breach takes precedence over an individual printer fault or routine request.
  • Assess the problem: The technician must check known faults, gather relevant information and follow approved troubleshooting instructions.
  • Verify identity: Before resetting passwords or changing access, the technician must verify the requester’s identity using AFC’s approved process.
  • Obtain approval: Access changes, purchases and configuration changes must follow the relevant approval procedures.
  • Resolve routine faults: The technician must perform authorised fixes and record the actions taken and results.
  • Escalate technical faults: Network problems must be referred to the Network Administrator. Server and account problems must be referred to the Systems Administrator.
  • Escalate major incidents: Widespread outages and suspected security incidents must be reported immediately to the IT Manager, who coordinates the response.
  • Coordinate branch support: Branch IT Liaisons must assist with local information and site access without making unauthorised technical changes.
  • Communicate progress: The help desk must keep affected employees informed of progress, delays and available workarounds.
  • Confirm restoration: The technician must test the solution and ask the employee to confirm that the service works before closing the ticket.
  • Document closure: The ticket must record the resolution, completion time and any follow-up work. Recurring faults must be referred for further investigation.
  • Review performance: The IT Manager must review unresolved tickets, recurring incidents and response performance. Support hours and response targets must be agreed with management.

BUSINESS IMPACT & URGENCY

Priority examples

These examples guide assessment. The help desk must consider the actual impact and urgency of each report.

Illustrative incident priorities
Priority Example Handling
Critical All offices lose access to Sage ERP, or an active security incident is suspected. Notify the IT Manager immediately and coordinate the response.
High A branch loses connectivity and essential business activities cannot continue. Prioritise specialist investigation and assess available workarounds.
Normal One employee has a device or printer problem and a suitable workaround is available. Log, assess and resolve according to the agreed support targets.
