FROM REQUEST TO PAYMENT

Acquisition procedures and approvals

  • Submit a request: The employee or department must submit a purchase request describing the item, business need, quantity and required delivery date.
  • Department approval: The relevant department or Branch Manager must confirm the need before referring the request to the IT Manager.
  • Technical assessment: The IT Manager must check compatibility, security, capacity and support requirements, and determine whether existing equipment can meet the need.
  • Prepare specifications: IT must document the required technical features, warranties, licences, installation and support services.
  • Obtain quotations: The designated purchasing officer must obtain quotations from suitable suppliers. This proposed procedure requires three written quotations where practical. Exceptions must be justified and approved.
  • Compare suppliers: IT and purchasing staff must evaluate technical suitability, total cost, delivery time, warranty and ongoing support.
  • Check the budget: Finance must confirm available funds and account for equipment, installation, subscriptions, renewals and maintenance costs.
  • Authorise expenditure: The purchase must receive approval under AFC’s authorised spending limits. Requests outside delegated limits must be referred to the Chief Operations Officer or designated authority.
  • Issue a purchase order: Only authorised purchasing staff may place an order after approval. Employees must not commit AFC to purchases beforehand.
  • Receive and inspect: The receiving officer and IT staff must check delivered items against the purchase order and record missing, damaged or incorrect items.
  • Record assets: IT must record equipment serial numbers, asset tags, locations, warranties and assigned users. Software licences and subscription renewal dates must also be recorded.
  • Configure and test: Authorised IT staff must apply security settings, test compatibility and confirm that the purchase meets the approved requirements.
  • Approve payment: Finance must match the invoice to the purchase order and receipt or acceptance record before payment.
  • Retain records: Requests, quotations, approvals, orders, invoices and acceptance records must be retained under AFC’s records-management requirements.
  • Urgent purchases: Emergency requests require documented justification and approval from the designated authority before ordering.
  • Review: The IT Manager and Finance must review this procedure annually and when purchasing requirements change.

BEFORE AN ORDER IS PLACED

Approval checklist

  • The business need has been confirmed.
  • IT has checked the technical requirements.
  • Supplier quotations have been evaluated.
  • Finance has confirmed the budget.
  • The designated authority has approved expenditure.
  • An authorised purchase order has been issued.
