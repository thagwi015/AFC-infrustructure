USING AFC RESOURCES

Policy guidelines

  • Business use: Company computers, networks, internet connections and email accounts must primarily support authorised business activities.
  • Limited personal use: Occasional personal use may be permitted if it does not interfere with work, create security risks or generate additional costs.
  • Account responsibility: Users must use their assigned accounts and must not share passwords or allow others to work through their accounts.
  • Unauthorised access: Users must not access another person’s files, email or restricted systems without authorisation.
  • Email conduct: Company email must be used professionally. Sending threatening, discriminatory, harassing or offensive messages is prohibited.
  • Internet use: Users must not access, download or distribute unlawful material or use company resources for fraud, harassment or other illegal activities.
  • Suspicious content: Users must avoid opening unexpected attachments or links and report suspicious messages to the help desk.
  • Software installation: Users must not install unapproved software, browser extensions or pirated applications.
  • Company information: Confidential business, customer and employee information must not be uploaded to personal cloud accounts or unapproved online services.
  • External devices: Personal devices and removable storage may only connect to company systems with IT approval and the required security checks.
  • Network controls: Users must not disable security software, bypass firewall restrictions or change network settings without authorisation.
  • Responsible resource use: Downloads, streaming and printing must not disrupt business services or waste company resources.
  • Remote working: Users must follow AFC’s security policy and use approved methods when accessing company systems away from the office.
  • Reporting problems: Lost equipment, accidental disclosure of information and suspected misuse must be reported immediately to the help desk.
  • Monitoring: AFC may monitor the use of its IT resources through authorised staff, subject to applicable requirements and communicated company procedures.
  • Policy breaches: Suspected misuse must be investigated and referred to management under AFC’s disciplinary procedures.
  • Acknowledgement and review: Users must acknowledge this policy before receiving access. The IT Manager must review it annually and when requirements change.
