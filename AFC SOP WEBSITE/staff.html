HOW WE ARE ORGANISED

Reporting structure

The IT Manager reports to the Chief Operations Officer. The technical support team reports to the IT Manager.

Chief Operations Officer Business oversight

Systems Administrator Reports to the IT Manager
Help Desk Technician Reports to the IT Manager
Branch IT Liaisons

Report to their Branch Managers and coordinate IT matters with the IT Manager. This duty may be assigned to an existing employee at each regional branch.

WHO DOES WHAT?

Roles and responsibilities

Proposed operational roles for AFC
Position Main responsibilities Reports to

GETTING THE RIGHT SUPPORT

Fault reporting and escalation

  1. Report the problem. Employees contact the help desk, which records the issue.
  2. Refer specialist faults. Network faults go to the Network Administrator. Server and account problems go to the Systems Administrator.
  3. Escalate major incidents. The IT Manager coordinates serious disruptions and requests requiring management approval.
  4. Inform business leadership. The IT Manager reports significant operational issues to the Chief Operations Officer.

Branch IT Liaisons assist with local coordination but do not authorise technical changes.
